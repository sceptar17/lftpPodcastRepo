Stop Crying! Loving God Without Weeping.

Where Do I Start?
11/1/2010

Let's say you or someone you know is God curious. You feel like He might exist or you're interested in a closer relationship with Him and you don't quite know what to do from there. Well, we're here to help.

Now, let's be clear, this is a personal relationship with God we're talking about so no amount of effort on our end will make a lick of difference as to how close you are to God. Anyone of this earth that puts themselves as an intermediary between you and God is a charlatan.

That being said, as Christ followers, we can share our experiences with you, provide relevant Biblical passages about having a relationship with Christ, and be encouraging and there to answer questions.

This will be "I'm Christ Curious 101 - Where Do I Start?" and we would invite you to come along with us and pass the invitation on to anyone you know that is thinking about starting or renewing a relationship with Jesus. I'm excited about this one, folks, and look forward to seeing you there.
